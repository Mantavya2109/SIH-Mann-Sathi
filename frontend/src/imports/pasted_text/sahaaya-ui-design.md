Design a polished, high-fidelity responsive web application UI for a government-backed AI-powered Dynamic Mental Health Monitoring and Distress Prediction System for victims and complainants involved in atrocity-related cases.

The product should feel SAFE, HUMAN, TRUSTWORTHY, MODERN, CALM, and ACCESSIBLE — not like a hospital management system and not like a generic corporate dashboard.

The platform continuously checks in with victims/complainants throughout investigation, trial, rehabilitation, compensation, and protection processes. It uses conversational AI, sentiment analysis, emotion AI, voice stress analytics, behavioural signals, predictive risk modelling, explainable AI, and automated alerts to help counsellors and authorized officers identify rising psychological distress early and provide appropriate intervention.

Create a cohesive design system and three high-fidelity desktop screens using the same visual language.

==================================================
DESIGN DIRECTION
================

Use a fresh, bright, reassuring visual identity.

Primary palette:

* Deep navy / indigo for trust and authority
* Bright teal or turquoise as the primary action/accent colour
* Soft sky blue
* Fresh mint/green for safe/stable states
* Warm amber for moderate risk
* Restrained coral/red only for high-risk alerts
* Warm off-white / very light blue-gray backgrounds
* White cards

Avoid excessive red, dark gloomy colours, gradients that look flashy, neon colours, or stereotypical medical/hospital aesthetics.

Use generous whitespace, rounded cards, subtle borders, soft shadows, clean modern typography, accessible contrast, and a calm visual hierarchy.

Typography should feel contemporary and highly readable, similar to Inter, Manrope, or another clean modern sans-serif.

Use 12–16px corner radii for cards and controls.

Use simple line icons with consistent stroke weight.

The interface should feel appropriate for an Indian government/social justice context while still looking like a modern 2026 digital product.

Do NOT use imagery depicting violence, trauma, crying victims, police weapons, courtrooms, blood, or distressing situations.

Instead use subtle human-centered illustrations, abstract supportive shapes, community/support imagery, or calm geometric illustrations.

==================================================
UX PRINCIPLES
=============

1. Victim-facing UI must be extremely simple and calming.
2. Never overwhelm the victim with statistics, risk scores, legal terminology, or complicated navigation.
3. The counsellor interface can expose advanced AI analytics.
4. Distress scores should be presented as decision-support signals, not medical diagnoses.
5. Always provide human support as an alternative to AI.
6. Privacy and confidentiality should be visible throughout the experience.
7. Avoid exposing unnecessary personally identifiable information.
8. Make emergency/help access easy but not visually alarming.
9. Use progressive disclosure for complex AI explanations.
10. Design for multilingual expansion, especially Indian languages.
11. Ensure large touch targets and readable typography.
12. Use inclusive, respectful language throughout.

Overall visual impression:

“Government-grade trust + modern AI product + human-centered mental health support.”

The final result should look polished enough for a Smart India Hackathon (SIH) prototype presentation and should clearly communicate the innovation of continuous AI-powered distress monitoring without making the victim feel like they are being clinically monitored or judged.
